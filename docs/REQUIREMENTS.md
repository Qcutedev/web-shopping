# Requirements

Single source of truth for what the marketplace must do. Owner: product-requirements agent.

Priority: `MVP` (first release), `LATER` (designed for, not built), `OUT` (not planned).

Where a rule or criterion carries a tag such as "(open: Q42)", the behaviour is not yet decided. It must not be built on a guess; see [Open questions](#open-questions-awaiting-the-owner). What the documents state until the owner answers is in the "Meanwhile" column there.

## Roles

| Role | How obtained |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | Not logged in. Can browse, search, and view products and stores. Cannot hold a cart (Q5). |
| Customer | Self-registration. |
| Seller | A customer whose seller application an admin approved. Keeps customer abilities. |
| Admin | Bootstrap script only (REQ-A-11, BR-26). The script may be run again to create further admins. No admin screen or endpoint grants the admin role in the MVP. |

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The product's name. "Product name" and "title" mean the same field. |
| Visible | A product is visible on the storefront when every visibility condition in BR-05 holds. Stock does not affect visibility: a visible product with zero stock is sold out and stays visible (BR-24). |
| Not visible (hidden) | A product that fails any visibility condition in BR-05. It appears in no storefront listing or search result, its product page returns 404 to the public, and it cannot be added to a cart or bought. |
| Sold out | A visible product whose stock is 0. Marked "สินค้าหมด" (BR-24). |
| On sale | Used for the seller's stock figures only (BR-25): product status `PUBLISHED` and moderation status `APPROVED`. A published product still awaiting approval is not on sale. A published, approved product stays on sale in this sense while its store is suspended or its category is inactive. |
| Cannot be bought now | Said of a cart line whose product is not visible (BR-05) or is sold out. The line stays in the cart, is marked, and is left out of the totals (BR-38). The mark is "สินค้าหมด" on a sold-out line and "สินค้านี้ไม่พร้อมจำหน่ายในขณะนี้" on a line whose product is not visible. |
| Paid time | The moment an order's payment became `PAID`. One per order; an order that is not paid has none (BR-39). "Paid date" means the calendar day in Asia/Bangkok on which the paid time falls. |
| Low stock | Stock of 1 to 5 on a product that is on sale (BR-25). A sold-out product is not low stock; it is counted in a separate figure. |
| Purchasable | Visible, and stock is at least the requested quantity (BR-05). |
| Store owner | The user account that owns a store (BR-06). |

## Business rules

| Id | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| BR-01 | Money is stored and computed in integer satang. Display is THB with two decimals. |
| BR-02 | Product unit price: 1.00 to 1,000,000.00 THB. |
| BR-03 | Quantity per cart line: 1 to 99, and never above current stock at checkout. |
| BR-04 | Order grand total may not exceed 20,000,000.00 THB (keeps totals inside a 32-bit integer of satang). |
| BR-05 | Visibility rule. A product is visible on the storefront only when all five conditions hold: (1) product status `PUBLISHED`; (2) moderation status `APPROVED`, so a product awaiting first approval, awaiting re-approval after an edit (BR-18), rejected, or taken down is not visible; (3) store status `APPROVED`; (4) the store owner's user account is active (BR-27); (5) the product's category is active (BR-23). Stock is not a visibility condition: a product that meets all five conditions with stock 0 stays visible and is marked "สินค้าหมด" (BR-24). A product that is not visible appears in no homepage, search, category, or store listing, its product page returns 404 to the public, and it cannot be added to a cart or bought. A product is purchasable only when it is visible and its stock is at least the requested quantity. |
| BR-06 | One store per seller account. |
| BR-07 | Checkout splits the cart into one seller order per store. Each seller order has its own fulfilment status and shipping fee. |
| BR-08 | Shipping fee is a flat fee per seller order, taken from marketplace settings. The initial value is 40.00 THB (4,000 satang) per seller order (Q1). An admin may change it (REQ-A-09); a change applies only to orders created afterwards. The fee is labelled as a simulated rate wherever it is shown. |
| BR-09 | Order items snapshot product title, SKU, unit price, and primary image at purchase. Orders snapshot the shipping address. Seller orders snapshot the store name, the shipping fee, and the commission rate. Later edits never change history: after a store is renamed, every order placed before the rename still shows the store name as it was at purchase, to the customer, the seller, and the admin (D-025, OQ-18). |
| BR-10 | Payment status and fulfilment status are separate. See the state tables below. |
| BR-11 | A customer may review a product once per purchased order item, only after that item's seller order is `DELIVERED`. |
| BR-12 | Products referenced by any order are archived, never hard-deleted. A product that no order references is hard-deleted when its seller deletes it; it then disappears from every customer's cart, and those customers are shown no message about it. This is accepted for the MVP (D-026, OQ-43). |
| BR-13 | Commission rate is stored in settings and recorded on each seller order at purchase time. The initial value is 5 percent (Q3). It is recorded only: nothing is deducted from any amount, and no payout is calculated in the MVP. |
| BR-14 | Suspending a store hides its products from the storefront and blocks new orders for it. Existing orders remain visible to the seller and the customer and can still be fulfilled by the seller: the seller of a suspended store can still log in, list and read the store's seller orders, and move them through the fulfilment table (REQ-S-07, REQ-S-08; D-027). Everything else is view only, until the store is reinstated: the seller may read their own products, store settings, and dashboard summary. Every request to create or edit a product, adjust stock, publish, unpublish, or archive a product is refused and changes nothing (D-028). Every request to delete a product, upload a picture, or change the store's settings (name, description, logo, contact phone) is also refused and changes nothing: the owner confirmed that "view only" covers these three (D-033 point 3, Q39). |
| BR-15 | Stock is decremented when the order is created. Stock is restored when the payment becomes `FAILED`, and when a seller order is moved to `CANCELLED`. Stock for one order item is restored at most once across all paths (D-023). |
| BR-16 | A seller cannot buy from their own store. |
| BR-17 | Product approval is always required. There is no setting, switch, or request that turns approval off or lets a product become visible without an admin approving it. Marketplace settings contain no moderation on/off option (D-025, OQ-04). |
| BR-18 | Re-approval after an edit. When a seller changes the title, description, category, or pictures of a product whose moderation status is `APPROVED`, its moderation status returns to `PENDING`, it enters the admin approval queue (BR-36), and it is not visible (BR-05) until an admin approves it again. Only those four fields trigger re-approval. A change to price or stock (and to any other field) leaves the moderation status unchanged and takes effect at once: the new price or stock is what the storefront shows and what checkout uses from that moment. A save in which none of the four fields differs from the stored value does not trigger re-approval (D-025, OQ-05). A change to "pictures" means that a picture was added, removed, or replaced. Changing only the order of the existing pictures, or which of them is shown first, is not such a change: the moderation status stays `APPROVED`, the product stays visible, and the new order is what the storefront shows at once (D-026, OQ-35). A change of category to an inactive category is refused (BR-23). |
| BR-19 | A product that an admin rejected or took down is not final. The seller who owns it can correct it, and saving the correction sends it back for approval automatically: when the seller saves a change to its title, description, category, pictures (a picture added, removed, or replaced), or price, its moderation status becomes `PENDING`, it enters the admin approval queue (BR-36), and it stays not visible until an admin approves it (D-025, OQ-06). There is no separate "submit again" action, and a product cannot be sent back unchanged: a save in which no field differs from the stored value leaves it rejected or taken down and puts nothing in the queue (D-026, OQ-36). A save that changes only the stock, the SKU, or the order of the pictures does not send the product back: the change is stored and the product stays rejected or taken down. Price counts as a correction only here; a price change to an `APPROVED` product never triggers re-approval (BR-18) (D-028, Q31). |
| BR-20 | Failed payment. When an order's payment becomes `FAILED`, whether the mock provider declined it or the order was still awaiting payment 15 minutes after it was created (the two cases are treated exactly alike: D-026, OQ-27; the 15 minutes: D-028): (1) stock is restored (BR-15); (2) the items of the order are returned to the customer's cart, combined with what the cart already holds as BR-32 says, except the items of a per-store part that was already cancelled before the payment failed: those do not return, and their stock was already restored by the cancellation (D-028, Q33); (3) the order stays `FAILED` permanently: no request pays, retries, or reopens it, and the customer buys the items only by checking out again, which creates a new order; (4) sellers do not see the order: its seller orders appear in no seller order list, a seller's request for one of them by id returns 404, and they are counted in no seller dashboard figure (D-025, OQ-07); (5) the order stays visible to the customer who placed it, in their order history and on its detail page, and to the admin, in the order list and on its detail page. In each of those places the order and each of its per-store parts is marked "ชำระเงินไม่สำเร็จ" (D-028, Q35), and never "ยกเลิกแล้ว คืนเงินนอกระบบ"; (6) no further action is possible on it: no pay, retry, cancel, or fulfilment action is offered for it, and any request by an admin to change the fulfilment status of one of its seller orders is rejected and changes nothing (D-026, OQ-25). Timeout: an order is never failed by timeout before 15 minutes have passed since it was created; once they have passed and its payment is still `PENDING`, the timeout check fails it with the effects above. |
| BR-21 | Cancelling a paid order. A seller (own seller orders only) or an admin may cancel a seller order whose order payment is `PAID`, while its fulfilment status is `PENDING` or `PROCESSING`. The cancelled seller order is displayed as "ยกเลิกแล้ว คืนเงินนอกระบบ" wherever its status is shown. The system records no refund: the payment status stays `PAID` and no refund record or amount is created. A cancelled seller order is not counted in any sales or revenue figure (REQ-S-03, REQ-A-08, BR-33) (D-025, OQ-08). |
| BR-22 | No partial purchase. Checkout creates an order only when every cart line can be bought at its quantity. If any line cannot (the product is not visible under BR-05, or its stock is below the line's quantity), the whole checkout stops: no order is created, no stock changes, nothing is charged, and the response identifies each such line and the reason (D-025, OQ-09). Two cases. (a) The line cannot be bought now, because the product is not visible or is sold out (stock 0): the line stays in the cart as it is (BR-38), and checkout keeps stopping until the customer removes the line or the product can be bought again. (b) The line's quantity is above the stock and the stock is at least 1, found at the moment the order is placed: the stopped checkout request itself stores the lowered quantity (the stock) in the cart line, the response gives the lowered quantity and the totals recomputed with it, and the customer confirms by submitting checkout again. That second submission, with stock unchanged, creates the order for the lowered quantity (D-026, OQ-30; D-029). A checkout whose cart has lines of both kinds stops once and reports all of them. |
| BR-23 | Inactive category. While a category is inactive, every product in it is not visible (BR-05): it is absent from the homepage, search results, category pages, and store pages, its product page returns 404 to the public, and it cannot be added to a cart or bought (D-025, OQ-10). The effect lasts only while the category is inactive: when the category is switched on again, its products become visible again by themselves, with no action by a seller or an admin, provided the other conditions of BR-05 hold (D-026, OQ-31). A seller cannot create a product in an inactive category or move a product to one: the request is refused, and nothing is created or changed. Editing the other fields of a product that is already in an inactive category is allowed (D-026, OQ-39). |
| BR-24 | Sold-out products stay visible. A product that meets the visibility conditions of BR-05 and has stock 0 still appears in listings, search, and on its product page, and is marked "สินค้าหมด" in each of those places. It cannot be bought while its stock is 0 (D-025, OQ-11). |
| BR-25 | "Low stock" means stock of 1 to 5 inclusive. A sold-out product (stock 0) is not low stock: sold-out products are shown as a separate figure. Both figures count only products that are on sale, meaning product status `PUBLISHED` and moderation status `APPROVED`. A draft, archived, rejected, taken-down, or awaiting-approval product is counted in neither figure, whatever its stock. This is the only definition of low stock in the product (D-025, OQ-12; D-026, OQ-33). The two figures look only at the product's own two statuses: a published, approved product is still counted while it is hidden because its store is suspended or its category is inactive (D-028, Q32). |
| BR-26 | In the MVP an admin account is created only by the bootstrap script (REQ-A-11). No admin screen and no endpoint gives a user the admin role (D-025, OQ-13). The script is not limited to the first admin: it may be run again to create a further admin account (D-026, OQ-37). When the script is given an email address that already has an account: if that account is already an admin, the script finishes without changing anything; if it is a customer or a seller, the script refuses and changes nothing. The script never promotes an existing account: an admin is always an account the script created (D-028, Q37). |
| BR-27 | Deactivated store owner. While the user account that owns a store is deactivated (REQ-A-02), the store and all of its products are hidden: the store page returns 404 to the public, its products are not visible (BR-05), and they cannot be added to a cart or bought (D-025, OQ-14). The effect lasts only while the owner is deactivated: when the owner is reactivated, the store and its products come back by themselves, with no further action, in the state they were in before (a store that was suspended is still suspended, and each product is visible only if the other conditions of BR-05 hold) (D-026, OQ-31). Deactivating the owner changes no order: the store's seller orders keep their status, nothing is cancelled automatically, and no stock moves. While the owner is deactivated nobody can move those seller orders to `PROCESSING` or `SHIPPED`; an admin cancels the paid ones by hand, one seller order at a time (REQ-A-07, BR-21) (D-026, OQ-32). |
| BR-28 | Limits on marketplace settings. Flat shipping fee: 0.00 to 1,000.00 THB inclusive (0 to 100,000 satang). Commission rate: a whole number of percent from 0 to 100 inclusive; a value with a decimal part (for example 2.5) is rejected (D-026, OQ-38). A value outside its range is rejected and the setting keeps its previous value (D-025, OQ-16). |
| BR-29 | A seller order whose fulfilment status is `SHIPPED` can only become `DELIVERED`. It cannot be cancelled, and there is no "lost" status or action in the MVP (D-025, OQ-17). |
| BR-30 | Quantity above stock. When a product's stock has dropped below a quantity the customer already chose (in a cart line or in the quantity selector on a product page) and the stock is at least 1, the quantity is lowered automatically to the stock available and the customer is shown a message saying that it was lowered (D-025, OQ-23). Reading never stores anything: a read of the cart or the checkout page reports the line with the lowered quantity and the message, and leaves the stored quantity as it was; the lowered quantity is stored by the next request that changes the cart for that line (a set-quantity or add request under BR-31, or the stopped checkout request of BR-22) (D-029). The message is exactly "สินค้าเหลือ {available} ชิ้น ระบบปรับจำนวนเป็น {available} ชิ้นให้แล้ว", with both placeholders replaced by the stock available, shown once for each affected line (D-026, OQ-46). The customer is told in every place where the lowering is met: at the quantity selector on the product page, on the cart page, on the checkout page, and in the result of a checkout that stopped because of it (BR-22) (D-026, OQ-30). The line total and every total shown use the lowered quantity. The customer is never left holding a quantity above stock without being told. When the stock is 0 the quantity is not lowered: the product is sold out and BR-38 applies. |
| BR-31 | Adding to the cart against stock. (1) A request to add a product whose stock is 0 is refused: no cart line is created or changed, and the customer is shown "สินค้าหมด ไม่สามารถเพิ่มลงตะกร้าได้" (D-028, Q35). (2) A request to add a product, or to set a line's quantity, that would make the line's quantity higher than the product's stock, with stock at least 1, is not refused: the line's quantity becomes the stock, and the customer is shown the BR-30 message for that line. (3) The 1 to 99 limit comes first: a requested quantity outside 1 to 99, or an add that would bring an existing line above 99, is rejected and leaves the line unchanged (BR-03), whatever the stock (D-026, OQ-28). |
| BR-32 | Returned items meeting the cart. When the items of a failed payment return to the cart (BR-20) and the cart has no line for a product, a line is created with the order item's quantity. When the cart already has a line for the product, the two quantities are added together. The result is capped at 99 and at the product's stock as it stands after the restore of BR-15, whichever is lower. A returned product that is no longer visible (BR-05) still returns; its line is flagged as not buyable like any other such line. When a cap made the quantity lower than the sum and the payment failed during the customer's own request (a simulated decline), the result of that request names each capped line and the customer is shown, for each, "ปรับจำนวนในตะกร้าเป็น {quantity} ชิ้น", with the placeholder replaced by the quantity the cart line now holds (D-026, OQ-26; D-028, Q35). When the payment failed by timeout, no message is required, then or later: the cart showing the quantity it really holds is enough (D-028, Q34). Only the items that return under BR-20 are combined: items of a part cancelled before the failure are not added to the cart. |
| BR-33 | Sales figures and order counts. (1) Every sales or revenue amount (the seller's paid sales total, REQ-S-03; the admin's paid revenue, REQ-A-08) is the sum of the price of the goods: the item line totals, at the unit prices recorded at purchase, of the seller orders that count. The shipping fee is never part of it. (2) A seller order counts when its order's payment is `PAID` and its fulfilment status is not `CANCELLED` (BR-21). (3) The admin report's order count counts an order once when its payment is `PAID` and at least one of its seller orders is not `CANCELLED`. An order with only some stores' parts cancelled therefore counts as one order; an order whose parts are all cancelled, and an order whose payment is `PENDING` or `FAILED`, is not counted (D-026, OQ-34). |
| BR-34 | Stock limit. The largest stock quantity a seller may enter for one product is 1,000,000. A seller request that sets the stock above 1,000,000 (when creating or editing a product, or setting an absolute value), or an increase by a delta whose result would be above 1,000,000, is rejected and the stock is unchanged; exactly 1,000,000 is accepted. The limit applies only to what a seller enters: stock restored by a failed payment or a cancellation (BR-15) is never refused because of it, and a seller's decrease is accepted even when the stock stands above 1,000,000 after such a restore (D-026, OQ-40). |
| BR-35 | A seller sees an order only once it is paid. While an order's payment is `PENDING`, and for ever once it is `FAILED` (BR-20), its seller orders are absent from every seller order list, a seller's request for one of them by id (to read it or to change its status) returns 404, and they are counted in no seller dashboard figure. From the moment the payment is `PAID` the seller order appears (D-026, OQ-41). |
| BR-36 | Admin approval queue. The queue lists exactly the products whose moderation status is `PENDING` and whose product status is `PUBLISHED`. A product the seller has saved but not yet published is not in the queue; it enters the queue when the seller publishes it, and leaves the queue without a decision if the seller unpublishes or archives it first (D-026, OQ-42). Order: oldest first by submission time, which is the moment the product most recently entered the queue. Publishing a `PENDING` product sets that time to the moment of publishing, so a product that sat as a draft for a long time does not go ahead of products already waiting. Decisions: an admin can approve or reject only a product that is in the queue. A request to approve or reject a product the seller has not published, also when the admin opens it directly by id, is rejected and changes nothing. Taking down an `APPROVED` product is unchanged (D-028). |
| BR-37 | Order numbers. Every order has a unique order number, shown to the customer, the seller, and the admin. Order numbers may count upwards; that the number of orders can be estimated from them is accepted for the MVP, and nothing requires them to be unpredictable. An order number never gives access: another customer's order returns 404 whichever number or id is supplied (REQ-C-13) (D-026, OQ-44). |
| BR-38 | Cart line that cannot be bought now. Sold out: when a product that is already in a cart reaches stock 0, its cart line is kept: the system never removes it. The line is marked "สินค้าหมด", is not counted in the per-store totals or the grand total, cannot be bought, and blocks checkout under BR-22 until the customer removes it. A request to change the line's quantity while the stock is 0 is refused and leaves the line unchanged; removing the line is always allowed. If the stock later returns to 1 or more, the line can be bought again, and BR-30 applies if its quantity is above the new stock (D-026, OQ-29). Hidden: a cart line whose product is no longer visible under BR-05 (for example taken down, awaiting re-approval, store suspended, store owner deactivated, or category inactive) is treated in the same way: it stays, is marked as not buyable now, is not counted in the per-store totals or the grand total, blocks checkout, refuses a quantity change, can always be removed, and can be bought again if the product becomes visible again (D-028, Q36). The mark on a hidden line is exactly "สินค้านี้ไม่พร้อมจำหน่ายในขณะนี้" (D-033 point 2, Q38). It is the same text whichever condition of BR-05 hides the product: the mark never says why the product is hidden, and no other text on the line or in the cart names the reason (taken down, awaiting approval, store suspended, store owner deactivated, or category inactive). A hidden line is never marked "สินค้าหมด", also when the hidden product's stock is 0: sold out is said only of a visible product (Terms; [API_CONTRACTS.md](API_CONTRACTS.md) cart section, where a hidden line is never answered as sold out). When the product becomes visible again the hidden mark is gone, and the line is then marked "สินค้าหมด" if its stock is 0. The text is a customer-facing string and is kept with the other Thai strings (REQ-N-01). |
| BR-39 | Paid time. An order has at most one paid time: the moment its payment became `PAID`. It is recorded at that moment and never changes afterwards. An order whose payment is `PENDING` or `FAILED` has no paid time. Cancelling a seller order after payment leaves the payment `PAID` (BR-21) and so leaves the paid time as it was. The paid time belongs to the order as a whole: a seller order has no payment and no paid time of its own, so every store's part of one order has the same paid time. Shown: the order detail page of the customer (REQ-C-13), of the seller (REQ-S-07), and of the admin (REQ-A-07) shows the paid time of a paid order (D-033 point 4, Q40). For an order with no paid time nothing is shown as a paid time: no date, not the creation time, and no zero date or other placeholder date. The paid time is stored in UTC and displayed as a date and a time of day in the `th-TH` locale and the Asia/Bangkok timezone (REQ-N-01). Example: an order paid at 2026-10-11T18:30:00Z is shown as paid on 12 October at 01:30, not on 11 October. The exact display format (seconds or not, which year numbering) is open (open: Q43). Whether pages other than the three detail pages also show the paid time is open (open: Q42). |
| BR-40 | Admin filter by paid date. The admin order list (REQ-A-07) can be filtered by the date an order was paid, so that the list can be matched against the sales report, which places an order by its paid date (REQ-A-08) (D-033 point 4, Q40). (1) Range. The range is bounded by the same rule as the report's range ([API_CONTRACTS.md](API_CONTRACTS.md) section 1.6 and the report in its admin section): the admin picks calendar days in Asia/Bangkok, each picked day counts whole, and the first and the last day are both included. The range therefore runs from 00:00 Bangkok time on the first day up to, and not including, 00:00 Bangkok time on the day after the last day. A range of one day is that whole day. Example: the range 11 October 2026 to 11 October 2026 lists the orders whose paid time is at or after 2026-10-10T17:00:00Z and before 2026-10-11T17:00:00Z; an order paid at 2026-10-11T16:59:59Z is listed, and an order paid at 2026-10-11T17:00:00Z is not listed and is listed for 12 October. (2) Which orders. With the filter in use, the list holds only orders whose paid time is inside the range. An order whose payment is `PENDING` or `FAILED` has no paid time (BR-39) and is never listed while the filter is in use, whatever the range. (3) Match with the report. For one and the same range, every order that the report's order count counts (BR-33) is in the filtered list. The list can hold more than that count: a paid order whose every part is cancelled has a paid time and is listed, and the report does not count it (BR-33). (4) Other filters. The paid-date filter is separate from the filter on the date an order was created and does not replace it; an order created on 11 October at 23:58 Bangkok time and paid on 12 October at 00:03 is listed for paid date 12 October and for creation date 11 October. When the paid-date filter is used with another filter, an order is listed only when it passes every filter in use. (5) Refusals. A range whose last day is before its first day is rejected as invalid input and lists nothing. Only an admin can use the filter: a guest gets 401, and a customer or a seller gets 403 (REQ-A-01). (6) Scope. The filter is required on the admin order list only: the customer's order history and the seller's order list are not required to filter by paid date. Whether the range may be given with only one end is open (open: Q44). |
| BR-41 | Admin read of product pictures (SEC-090; found in the T-051 review). Pictures are moderated content (BR-17, BR-18, BR-19), so an admin must be able to see every picture of a product they can open. (1) Who. A request for a stored file made with the session of an active `ADMIN` is served when the file is attached to a product as a picture, whatever that product's status, moderation status, or store, and whether or not the product is visible (BR-05). This is the same set of products as the admin product detail ("any product, whatever its state"). (2) Not covered. The admin rule adds nothing else: an upload that is attached to no product (not yet attached, or detached by a seller edit and now unreferenced), an orphan past its grace period (SEC-065), and the logo of a store whose page is not public stay unreadable by an admin. Files that are public (BR-05 pictures, order item pictures, logos of public stores) are served to everyone as before. (3) Everyone else unchanged. A customer, a guest, and a seller who does not own the file get the same `404` as for an unknown key; no extra status, header, or body tells the caller that the file exists. A deactivated admin has no session (REQ-A-02) and so has no access. (4) No write. Serving a file to an admin changes no row and writes no audit entry (REQ-A-10 lists only admin actions that change something); the bytes served, the content type, and the protective headers are the same as for any other caller. (5) Moderation. An approval decision must not be possible on pictures the admin could not see: the pictures of a queued product are readable by the admin who decides (REQ-A-05). |

### Payment status (per order)

| From | To | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PENDING` | `PAID` | Mock provider confirms |
| `PENDING` | `FAILED` | Mock provider declines (simulated failure) |
| `PENDING` | `FAILED` | Timeout: the order is still awaiting payment 15 minutes after it was created (D-023; D-026, OQ-27; D-028) |

Any other transition is rejected. The payment statuses are exactly `PENDING`, `PAID`, and `FAILED`. No refund status exists, because the system records no refund: cancelling a paid seller order leaves the payment `PAID` (BR-21). `PAID` and `FAILED` are final: a failed order is never paid again or retried, and its items return to the customer's cart (BR-20). A payment that fails by timeout is treated exactly like one that was declined. The timeout is 15 minutes from the creation of the order: an order whose payment is `PENDING` is not failed by timeout before that.

### Fulfilment status (per seller order)

| From | To | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PENDING` | `PROCESSING` | Seller, only when payment is `PAID` |
| `PROCESSING` | `SHIPPED` | Seller; tracking number and carrier name optional and entered by the seller |
| `SHIPPED` | `DELIVERED` | Seller or admin (simulated; no carrier confirmation) |
| `PENDING`, `PROCESSING` | `CANCELLED` | Admin, whether or not the order is already paid; seller, own seller orders, which a seller sees only once the order is paid (BR-21, BR-35); restores stock |
| `PENDING` | `CANCELLED` | System, when the order's payment becomes `FAILED` (BR-20); shown as "ชำระเงินไม่สำเร็จ", not as a cancellation |

Any other transition is rejected. In particular `SHIPPED` to `CANCELLED` is rejected and changes nothing, and no "lost" status exists (BR-29).

Customers cannot cancel their own orders in the MVP (Q4). No customer-facing cancel action exists, and any attempt by a customer to change a seller order's status is rejected and changes nothing.

A seller order that is cancelled after its order was paid is displayed as "ยกเลิกแล้ว คืนเงินนอกระบบ"; the payment stays `PAID` and no refund is recorded (BR-21).

Sellers do not see the seller orders of an order whose payment is `FAILED` or still `PENDING` (BR-20, BR-35). The seller orders of a failed order carry the fulfilment status `CANCELLED`, set by the system when the payment fails. The customer and the admin are shown them marked "ชำระเงินไม่สำเร็จ", never "ยกเลิกแล้ว คืนเงินนอกระบบ", and no status change is accepted for them (BR-20).

The seller of a suspended store can still read the store's seller orders and make every seller transition in the table above (BR-14).

Deactivating a store owner changes no fulfilment status. Paid seller orders of a deactivated owner are cancelled by an admin by hand; nothing is cancelled automatically (BR-27).

### Seller application and store status

A seller application and a store are two separate things, each with its own status. A store exists only once its application has been approved, so a store is never pending or rejected.

Seller application status: exactly `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, and `REJECTED`.

| From | To | Who | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new application) | `PENDING` | Customer | Only when the user owns no store (BR-06) and has no other `PENDING` application (REQ-S-01) |
| `PENDING` | `APPROVED` | Admin | The store is created with store status `APPROVED` and the user's role becomes `SELLER` (REQ-A-03) |
| `PENDING` | `REJECTED` | Admin, reason required | No store is created; the applicant stays a customer and may submit a new application |

Store status: exactly `APPROVED` and `SUSPENDED`.

| From | To | Who | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `APPROVED` | `SUSPENDED` | Admin, reason required | BR-14; the owner stays a seller |
| `SUSPENDED` | `APPROVED` | Admin, reason required | The store is reinstated |

Any other transition, of an application or of a store, is rejected.

### Product moderation

Products require admin approval before they go live (Q2), and approval can never be switched off (BR-17). A new product has moderation status `PENDING` and is not visible until an admin approves it. The admin approval queue holds a `PENDING` product only while the seller has it published, oldest submission first, and an admin can approve or reject only a product that is in the queue (BR-36).

| Event | Who | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product created | Seller | Moderation status `PENDING`; not visible; in the admin queue once the seller publishes it (BR-36) |
| Approve a `PENDING`, published product | Admin | `APPROVED`; visible if the other conditions of BR-05 hold |
| Reject a `PENDING`, published product, with reason | Admin | Rejected; not visible; the seller can correct and resubmit it (BR-19) |
| Approve or reject a product the seller has not published | Admin | Rejected request; nothing changes (BR-36) |
| Take down an `APPROVED` product, with reason | Admin | Taken down; not visible; the seller can correct and resubmit it (BR-19) |
| Change title, description, or category of an `APPROVED` product, or add, remove, or replace one of its pictures | Seller | Back to `PENDING`; not visible until approved again (BR-18) |
| Change price or stock of an `APPROVED` product | Seller | Stays `APPROVED`; the change takes effect at once (BR-18) |
| Change only the order of the pictures of an `APPROVED` product, or which one is first | Seller | Stays `APPROVED`; the change takes effect at once (BR-18) |
| Save a change to title, description, category, or price of a rejected or taken-down product, or add, remove, or replace one of its pictures | Seller | `PENDING` automatically, with no separate submit action; not visible until approved (BR-19) |
| Save a rejected or taken-down product with nothing changed, or with only stock, SKU, or picture order changed | Seller | Stays rejected or taken down; nothing enters the queue (BR-19) |
| Any product change by the seller of a suspended store | Seller | Refused; nothing changes (BR-14) |
| Create a product in, or move a product to, an inactive category | Seller | Refused; nothing is created or changed (BR-23) |

A seller can never set a product's moderation status to `APPROVED`: any seller request that tries is rejected and changes nothing.
